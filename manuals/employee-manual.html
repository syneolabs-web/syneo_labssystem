0 Welcome, Sign-In & Your Least-Privilege Access Scope

Syneolab PM is designed with Least-Privilege Role-Based Access Control (RBAC). This keeps your workspace clean, protects your personal timesheet and lead pipeline from other employees, and ensures you can focus 100% on your deliverables without administrative clutter.

Signing In & First-Time Setup

  • Have Credentials? Enter your work email and temporary password on the Sign In screen, then go to Settings → Profile to set your permanent 12+ character password.
  • Need an Account? Click Request access on the login screen, enter your Name, Email, Role/Title, and a short note. An Admin will approve your request and your login details will arrive via email.

Your Privacy Guarantee

  • Private Timesheet: Your past clock-in/out entries, break durations, and session notes are visible only to you and Workspace Admins.
  • Personal Lead Pipeline: Leads you capture or own are visible only to you and Workspace Admins.
  • Invited Meetings Only: You only see meetings where you are an invited attendee (or company-wide sessions).

1 Your Daily Workflow in 4 Simple Steps

Step 1: Clock In & Pick Your Focus Task

From Home or Time Clock, choose the task you are working on, add a quick note (e.g., "Building checkout API"), and click Clock in.

Step 2: Post Your 60-Second Standup

Open Standups & Goals, fill in Yesterday, Today, any Blockers, and your Mood, then click Share standup.

Step 3: Move Tasks & Flag Blockers Early

In Tasks, move your active work to In progress or In review, check off subtasks, attach your PR/Figma URL, or flag a blocker if you need help.

Step 4: Clock Out Cleanly

Use Pause for break during lunch, and click Clock out at the end of your workday so your 7-day attendance log stays accurate.

2 Quick Search (Ctrl+K) & Top Header Controls

  • Global Search (Ctrl + K or ⌘ + K): Press Ctrl + K anywhere in the app to instantly search for a task, project, lead, or meeting, or trigger quick actions like New Task or Add Lead.
  • Live Clock Pill (Top Right): Shows your live running work timer (in green) or break status (in amber) even while you browse other tabs.
  • Pomodoro Focus Pill (Top Right): When you start a Pomodoro sprint in the Time Clock, its live countdown (`24:59`) stays visible in the top header.
  • Notification Bell: Alerts you to overdue tasks assigned to you and meetings happening today.

3 Time Clock, Breaks, Pomodoro Timer & My Timesheet

The Time Clock tab helps you structure deep work sessions and keeps a transparent personal log of your hours.

Clocking In, Breaks & Clocking Out

  • Clock In: Select an active task from the dropdown (optional), enter what you are focusing on, and click Clock in.
  • Taking a Break: Click Pause for break when stepping away. Break time is tracked separately and automatically subtracted from your net worked duration. Click Resume work when you return.
  • Clock Out: Click the red Clock out button when your session ends.

Pomodoro Focus Timer & Personal Logs

  • Focus Sprints: Choose 25m Focus, 50m Deep, 5m Break, or 15m Recharge and click Start timer. You will receive a toast notification as soon as the sprint finishes.
  • My Timesheet & 7-Day Attendance: View your personal session history and your 7-day focus progress bar (measured against a standard 35h week). Click Export CSV to download a copy of your own time logs.

4 Posting Your Daily Standup & Viewing Team Goals

Daily standups replace long status calls with a quick written check-in.

  1. Navigate to Standups & Goals in the sidebar.
  2. At the top, review the Team Goals & OKRs cards to see how quarterly company targets are progressing.
  3. In the Check in for today card:
    • Select your current Mood / Energy (Focused, Energized, Steady, Stretched, or Blocked).
    • Fill in Yesterday (what you completed), Today (your main focus), and Blockers (leave as None if clear, or describe what you need).
  4. Click Share standup. If you need to update it later the same day, click Edit on your standup card.

5 Managing Your Tasks, Subtasks, Deliverables & Blockers

In Tasks, you can view all project tasks for team visibility and fully edit any task that you created or that an Admin assigned to you.

Finding & Moving Your Work

  • Click the Assigned to me filter chip to focus solely on your personal queue.
  • Use List view to change task status via the inline dropdown, or switch to Board view (Kanban) to advance cards across To do → In progress → In review → Done.
  • Click + New task anytime to create a personal task (automatically assigned to you).

Collaborating Inside a Task Card

  • Subtasks Checklist: Add granular steps (`+ Step`) and check them off as you complete each part of the deliverable.
  • Deliverable / PR / Figma URL: Paste the link to your pull request, design file, or document before moving the task to In review or Done.
  • Activity & Comments: Type updates or questions in the comment thread and click Post.
How to Flag a Blocker: If you are waiting on credentials, client feedback, or another dependency, open the task, check Flag this task as blocked, type what is needed in the text box, and click Save. This immediately highlights the task on the Admin's Home dashboard so they can unblock you fast.

6 Viewing Projects & Milestone Checklists Read-Only

Open Projects to see every active initiative across the company:

  • Each card displays the Project Title, Client, Status (`Planning`, `Active`, `Completed`), Overall Progress %, completed tasks counter, logged team hours vs. target capacity, and completed milestones.
  • Click View ↗ on any project card to read the full project description, target hours, due date, and milestone checklist.

7 Using the Multi-Color Calendar & Day Agenda

The Calendar tab gives you a color-coded monthly overview of your schedule:

  • Color-Coded Event Pills: Every meeting you are invited to, task due date, project deadline, and lead follow-up is displayed in a soft pastel rounded rectangle. Multiple events on the same day automatically use different pastel colors so you can distinguish them at a glance.
  • Day Agenda Panel: Click any date on the calendar grid to see all events for that day in the right-hand Agenda card. From there you can click into a task or click Join ↗ on a meeting.

8 Capturing & Managing Your Leads Personal Pipeline

The Leads tab is your personal CRM workspace. You only see leads that you created or that an Admin assigned to you.

Adding & Updating Leads

  • Click + Add lead to enter a prospect's Name/Title, Company, Email, Phone, Estimated Value, Stage, Follow-up Date, Lead Source, and Win Probability %.
  • Update the Stage dropdown directly in the table (`New` → `Contacted` → `Qualified` → `Proposal` → `Won` / `Lost`).
  • Use the Follow-up due tab to see prospects that need outreach today.

Fast Capture with the Lead Assistant

  • In the right-hand Lead assistant panel, type or paste raw notes from a call or email:
    Name: Maya Shah; Company: Acme; Email: maya@example.com; Value: 5000; Notes: Website redesign
  • Click Create lead draft ↗ — the assistant automatically parses the fields and opens a pre-filled Lead modal for you to review and save.

9 Joining Meetings & Syncing with Google Calendar

In Meetings, you see all upcoming and past sessions that you are invited to.

  • Joining a Call: Click Join meeting ↗ on any meeting card (or from the Calendar Agenda) to launch the live Google Meet or Instant Room video call in a new tab.
  • Adding to Your Google Calendar:
    • Automatic Email Invite (`.ics`): When an Admin schedules a meeting, you receive an email with a native Google Calendar invite attached — simply click Yes in Gmail to add it to your calendar.
    • 1-Click In-App Button: You can also click Open in Calendar ↗ or + Google Calendar on any meeting card in the app.

10 Announcements & Personalizing Your Settings

Company Announcements

  • Check Announcements in the sidebar to read official company updates, policy notes, and milestone celebrations.
  • Important items are marked with a purple Pinned tag at the top of the page.

Settings (Profile & Appearance)

  • Profile: View your account details, review your Role & Access Scope summary card, and update your password (12+ characters).
  • Appearance: Customize your workspace Accent Color (6 themes), preferred display Currency, and toggle Compact View for denser tables. Your preferences are saved automatically on your device.
