0 Executive Overview & Daily Management Rhythm

As an Administrator (role: admin), you have unrestricted visibility and control over every project, financial budget, employee timesheet, sales lead, meeting room, and workspace integration.

Morning Routine (5 Mins)

  • Open Home and review the Team Pulse & Attention Needed panel.
  • Check Blocked Tasks and Overdue Tasks to clear bottlenecks immediately.
  • Scan Standups & Goals to see who has checked in, their daily priorities, and energy levels.

Midday & Client Operations

  • Review Leads with Follow-up due tags; convert Won leads into active projects with 1 click (→ Project).
  • Schedule team or client calls in Meetings using Generate Google Meet + Everyone invite broadcast.

End-of-Week Review (10 Mins)

  • Open Time Clock → inspect the 7-Day Team Attendance & Focus table against the 35h weekly target.
  • Update Quarterly Goals (OKRs) in Standups & Goals and post a Friday milestone in Announcements.

2 Home (Executive Command Center)

Your Home view synthesizes delivery health, team attendance, and sales pipeline velocity into one screen.

4 Executive KPI Cards

  • Active Projects: Count of active engagements + total portfolio count.
  • Completion Rate: Percentage of tasks marked Done across the workspace.
  • Hours Tracked: Total team hours logged + live count of who is clocked in right now.
  • Open Leads: Active CRM opportunities + total pipeline value in your workspace currency.

Team Pulse & Attention Needed

  • Clocked In Now: Avatars and names of every employee currently working or on break.
  • Overdue Tasks: Tasks past their due date that are not yet Done.
  • Blocked Tasks: Tasks flagged by employees as blocked (click any item to open and resolve).

  • 7-Day Velocity: Bar chart of daily logged hours across the last 7 days.

3 Projects, Client Budgets & Milestones Admin Exclusive Write

Only Administrators can create, edit, or delete Projects, and only Administrators can view or edit the Client Budget field (which is stripped on the server for employees).

How to Create & Manage a Project

  1. Go to Projects in the left sidebar and click + New project.
  2. Fill in the Title, Description, Client name, and assign a Project Lead.
  3. Set Status (Planning, Active, or Completed) and Due date.
  4. Enter Target hours (capacity) (e.g., 120 hours) so the project card automatically tracks logged team hours against capacity.
  5. Enter Budget (visible only to Admins).
  6. Under Milestones, type each deliverable phase (e.g., Wireframes approved, Beta deployment, Final QA) and click + Add. Checking off milestones updates the milestone completion counter on the project card.
Automatic Progress Calculation: A project's progress percentage automatically updates based on the completion rate of tasks linked to that project or its completed milestones.

4 Tasks (Kanban Board, List View, Blockers & Broadcasts)

Tasks power daily execution. As an Admin, you can assign tasks to any employee, organize work by department, inspect blockers, and trigger Gmail notifications.

Filtering & Views

  • Status Tabs: Filter by All, To do, In progress, In review, or Done.
  • Scope & Department Filters: Filter by Assigned to me, High priority, or Department (Engineering, Design, Marketing, Management).
  • List vs. Board View: Toggle between the structured table (with inline status dropdowns) and the 4-column Kanban Board with 1-click stage movement buttons.

Inside the Task Modal

  • Estimated Hours & Deliverable URL: Set expected effort and attach a GitHub PR, Figma, or Google Doc link.
  • Blocker Flag: Check Flag this task as blocked and write what is needed to unblock it. Blocked tasks show a red Blocked badge across the app.
  • Subtasks & Activity Thread: Break tasks into granular checklist steps (+ Step) and post timestamped comments (Post).
  • Admin Email Controls: Check Email assignee via Gmail or Email Everyone on the team before clicking Save.

5 Multi-Color Calendar & Day Agenda

The Calendar unifies four workspace event types into a single visual schedule: Meetings, Task Due Dates, Project Deadlines, and Lead Follow-ups.

  • Distinct Pastel Event Pills: Every event is rendered in a soft rounded rectangle using a 6-color pastel palette (Sage, Sky Blue, Lavender, Amber, Rose, Teal). Multiple events on the same day are automatically assigned different colors so every item stands out clearly.
  • Interactive Day Selection: Click any date cell in the month grid to inspect its full schedule in the right-hand Day Agenda panel.
  • 1-Click Scheduling: Click + Schedule on [Date] in the right-hand agenda to open the Meeting scheduler pre-filled for that day.

6 Time Clock, Pomodoro Timer & Team Attendance

Track billable and operational hours across your entire organization while giving employees a distraction-free focus environment.

Live Clock & Pomodoro Focus

  • Select a task from Working on (optional), add a session note, and click Clock in.
  • Use Pause for break / Resume work to track break time separately from net worked hours.
  • Use the built-in Focus & Pomodoro Timer (25m Focus, 50m Deep, 5m Break, 15m Recharge) for structured sprints.

Admin Timesheet & 7-Day Attendance

  • Employee Filter Dropdown (Admin Only): Switch between All team members or any individual employee in the Team Timesheet card.
  • Team 7-Day Attendance & Focus Table: Compare every team member's live status, today's hours, 7-day total hours, sessions count, tasks completed, and visual progress bar against the 35h weekly benchmark.
  • Click Export CSV to download the complete team timesheet for payroll or client billing.

7 Daily Standups & Quarterly Goals (OKRs)

Keep the entire team aligned on high-level quarterly objectives and daily execution without unnecessary status meetings.

  • Team Goals & OKRs Admin Only Write: Click + New goal to define a quarterly objective (e.g., Close $50k in Q2 retainer leads), assign an owner, set Current value and Target value, and choose a status (On track, At risk, Behind, Completed). Use the +10% button on any goal card for rapid progress updates.
  • Daily Standup Check-in: Post what you completed yesterday, today's priority, any blockers, and your current energy/mood.
  • Active Blockers Banner: If any team member reports a blocker in their standup today, a highlighted alert card automatically appears under the check-in form so you can intervene early.

8 Leads CRM, Bulk CSV Import & 1-Click Project Conversion

As an Admin, you see the entire company's CRM pipeline across all sales reps and team members.

Pipeline Management & Conversion

  • Stage Progression: Move leads through New → Contacted → Qualified → Proposal → Won / Lost directly from the table dropdown.
  • Follow-up Alerts: Leads with a follow-up date of today or earlier automatically display a red Follow-up due badge and appear in the Follow-up due tab.
  • 1-Click → Project Button Admin Only: As soon as a lead is marked Won, click → Project to open a new Project modal pre-populated with the lead's company, value as budget, and notes.

Bulk CSV Import & Lead Assistant

  • Import CSV Admin Only: Click Import CSV to bulk-upload leads from a spreadsheet. Supported headers: Name (or Title), Company, Email, Phone, Value, Stage, Source, Notes, and Followup.
  • Lead Assistant Chat Capture: Paste unstructured notes in the right-hand chat box (e.g., Name: Maya Shah; Company: Acme; Email: maya@acme.com; Value: 5000; Notes: Mobile app) and click Create lead draft ↗.

9 Meetings, Live Google Meet Generation & Calendar Sync Admin Exclusive Write

Schedule meetings that automatically generate real Google Meet video rooms, sync to your Google Calendar, and send native .ics calendar invitations to attendees.

Step-by-Step: Scheduling a Synced Google Meet Session

  1. Go to Meetings and click + Schedule meeting.
  2. Enter the Title (e.g., Weekly Product & Delivery Sync), Start time, and End time.
  3. In the Meeting link section:
    • Click Generate Google Meet to call your connected Google Apps Script webhook and create a live https://meet.google.com/... link immediately, or leave the link blank with Auto-create Google Meet room & sync to Google Calendar checked so the server generates it upon saving.
    • Alternatively, click Generate Instant Room if you want a zero-login Jitsi video room.
  4. Under Attendees, click Everyone (All team) to select all active workspace members, or click individual member chips.
  5. Ensure Email calendar invite (.ics) to Everyone is checked and click Save.
What Happens When You Click Save: Every attendee receives a formatted HTML email with a 1-click Join Google Meet button, a + Add to Google Calendar link, and an attached invite.ics file (`METHOD:REQUEST`) that automatically renders native Yes / Maybe / No RSVP buttons in Gmail and Google Calendar.

10 Company Announcements Admin Exclusive Write

Broadcast official updates, policy changes, and team celebrations.

  • Click + Post announcement, enter a headline and message body, and choose a Category (General, Milestone, Policy, or Celebration).
  • Check Pin to top of announcements to keep critical notices at the top of the feed with a purple Pinned badge.
  • Check Email Everyone on the team to simultaneously deliver the announcement to every active member's inbox.

11 People, Access Requests & Least-Privilege RBAC Admin Only View

The People tab is visible exclusively to Administrators. From here you control who can enter the workspace and what role they hold.

Onboarding New Team Members

  • Option A — Approve Access Requests: When an employee clicks Request access on the login page, their request appears under Access requests. Click Approve to automatically create their account, generate a temporary password, and email their login credentials.
  • Option B — Direct Invite: Click + Invite member, enter their Name, Email, Title, Role (Member or Admin), and check Email login details via Gmail.

Managing Existing Accounts

  • Role Toggle: Click Make admin or Make member on any user row. (The system prevents demoting the last remaining Admin.)
  • Password Reset: Click Reset password to generate a fresh temporary password (displayed in a banner and emailed to the user).
  • Revoke Access: Click Remove to soft-delete a user and immediately invalidate all of their active login sessions.

Complete Server & UI Least-Privilege RBAC Matrix

Feature / Data Domain Administrator (admin) Employee (member) — Least Privilege
Projects & Budgets Full create/edit/delete; full visibility of client budget values. Read-only view of project briefs & milestones. Client budget is stripped by the server and hidden in UI.
Tasks Full CRUD across all tasks; can assign to anyone and trigger Gmail / Everyone broadcasts. Can create personal tasks and update status, subtasks, blockers, and comments on tasks assigned to them. Cannot edit others' tasks, reassign tasks, or send email broadcasts.
Time Clock & Timesheets View and export all employees' timesheets and 7-day attendance table. Can clock in/out and view/export only their own timesheet & 7-day attendance. Other employees' past logs & notes are redacted by the server.
Leads & CRM View all leads, bulk Import CSV, and convert Won leads → Project. Server returns only leads created by or assigned to that employee. Bulk CSV import and project conversion are hidden.
Meetings Schedule/edit/delete meetings, generate Google Meet links, email .ics invites to Everyone. Server returns only meetings the employee is invited to (or public sessions). Read-only details + 1-click Join & Add to Calendar.
Standups & OKRs Create/edit Quarterly Goals (OKRs) and view all daily standups. Post/edit own daily standups; read-only view of team standups and Quarterly Goals.
People & Integrations Full access to People directory, Access Requests, and Settings → Integrations. No access (hidden in UI and blocked with HTTP 403 on the server).

12 Settings, Integrations & Delivery Queue

Configure your personal preferences under Profile and Appearance, and manage workspace-wide webhooks under Integrations (Admin Only).

  • Profile: View your role scope summary and update your password (minimum 12 characters; updating automatically signs out old sessions).
  • Appearance: Choose from 6 executive accent colors (Botanical Green, Editorial Terracotta, Royal Indigo, Warm Amber,Rosewood, Ocean Teal), set your workspace display currency ($, €, ₹, £, AED), and toggle Compact Table Mode.
  • Integrations (Admin Only):
    • Gmail SMTP: Sends welcome credentials, task alerts, announcements, and meeting .ics invites. Click Send test email to me to verify.
    • Google Sheets Webhook: Mirrors new leads and time clock entries to your Google Sheet.
    • Google Calendar + Auto Google Meet Webhook: Connects your Google Apps Script deployment so meetings automatically create real meet.google.com links and sync to Google Calendar. Click Test Calendar & Meet sync to verify.
    • Delivery Queue: Inspect the 8 most recent background email/webhook jobs (`sent` or `failed`) and click Retry failed deliveries at any time.
